What is the PESO Verified badge?

Verifying your identity is completely optional. You can browse jobs, apply, and be hired without submitting an ID. Verifying just adds a PESO Verified badge to your profile so employers can see, at a glance, that your identity has been confirmed by PESO Iloilo City.

It doesn't unlock any extra features, and PESO can't see or edit your other resume details as part of the review — only your submitted ID against your name and birthday on file.
