Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

EasyHire is operated by PESO Iloilo City and complies with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the implementing rules issued by the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

1. What We Collect

When you create a jobseeker account, we collect:

  • Identifying information: name, date of birth, sex, civil status
  • Contact details: email address, phone, home address
  • Résumé data: education, work experience, skills, certifications, bio
  • Optional sector membership (LGBTQIA+, PWD, OSY, etc.) for priority-employment programs
  • Uploaded files: profile photo, valid ID (for personal-info changes), résumé PDF (optional)

For employer accounts, we additionally collect:

  • Company information: name, address, sector, recruitment contact
  • Representative information: name, position, contact details
  • Verification documents: Mayor's Permit, PhilJobNet accreditation, and other DOLE-required documents

2. How We Use It

Your personal information is used to:

  • Match jobseekers with relevant job opportunities and employers with qualified candidates
  • Display your profile to employers (visibility is controlled by your privacy settings)
  • Allow PESO administrators to verify and moderate accounts
  • Generate aggregate, anonymized labor-market analytics for PESO Iloilo City reporting
  • Send transactional emails: applications, interview schedules, password resets, announcements

3. Who Sees Your Information

Profile information is visible to verified employers actively recruiting through EasyHire. You can restrict this further in Settings:

  • Profile visibility — opt to hide your profile from employers after you are tagged as Hired
  • Sector badge visibility — opt to hide sector membership (e.g. PWD, LGBTQIA+) from employer-side views

PESO administrators have full access to all profiles and accounts for moderation and verification purposes. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties outside this platform.

4. Cookies & Session Storage

EasyHire uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember UI preferences (e.g. sidebar collapse state). We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Data Retention

Your account and résumé data are retained for as long as your account is active. If you deactivate your account from Settings, your profile is hidden from employers immediately and queued for deletion after a 30-day grace period. Aggregate analytics derived from your data (and stripped of identifiers) may be retained indefinitely.

6. Your Rights Under RA 10173

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about what data we hold on you
  • Access your personal data and request a copy
  • Request corrections via the Settings page (changes affecting identity are reviewed by PESO admins)
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent (by deactivating your account)
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission

7. Security

Passwords are stored using one-way cryptographic hashing. Connections to EasyHire are encrypted via HTTPS. Uploaded documents are stored on managed cloud infrastructure with access limited to PESO administrators. We follow reasonable industry practices to prevent unauthorized access, but no platform can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

EasyHire is intended for users aged 15 and above, consistent with the legal working age in the Philippines. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 15.

9. Updates to This Policy

Material changes to this policy will be announced via the in-app inbox and email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Data Protection Officer

For privacy-related concerns, contact PESO Iloilo City's Data Protection Officer at peso.iloilocity@gmail.com or by phone at (033) 337-0800.
