About EasyHire

The official digital platform of the Public Employment Service Office (PESO) of Iloilo City — connecting Ilonggos with meaningful local work.

For Jobseekers

Build a résumé once, get matched to every relevant opening. Filter by location, sector, and proximity to where you live.

For Employers

Post openings, review applications, and discover candidates whose skills, education, and experience actually fit the role.

PESO-verified

Every employer is reviewed by PESO Iloilo City before they can post. Look for the Verified badge.

How it works

1

Sign up. Pick whether you're looking for work or hiring. Fill in your basics — it takes a few minutes.

2

Match. Our matching engine weighs skills, education, experience, certifications, and proximity to find the best fits.

3

Apply. Send your application with a short message. Employers respond directly through EasyHire.

4

Get hired. When the employer marks you Hired, the role lands on your résumé automatically.
