Password Recovery

Three ways to get back into your account — try the automated reset first, and fall back to a PESO-mediated recovery if the email doesn't arrive or you no longer have access to the inbox on file.

{# ── Option 2: PESO-mediated (email) ──────────────────────────── #}

If the reset email doesn't arrive

Email PESO EasyHire Support with a valid ID. Response usually within 1–2 business days.

Compose email to easyhire.admin@gmail.com

Include in your email:

  • The email address registered to your EasyHire account
  • Your full name, exactly as it appears on the account
  • A clear photo or scan of any valid Philippine government ID so PESO staff can verify it's really you

PESO will reply with a temporary password. Sign in with it, then change your password immediately from Settings.

{# ── Option 3: In person ──────────────────────────────────────── #}

Or visit PESO Iloilo City in person

Bring your valid ID. Reset done at the counter.

Public Employment Service Office (PESO) — Iloilo City Hall
Plaza Libertad, Iloilo City
Hotline: (033) 337-0800

Note: fully-authenticated password-reset emails (no spam filtering) require a proper sender domain, which EasyHire will register when PESO Iloilo City adopts the platform. Until then, PESO-mediated recovery remains available as a robust fallback — matching how PhilHealth, SSS, and GSIS handle account recovery today.
