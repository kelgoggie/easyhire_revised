Terms & Conditions

Last updated: June 16, 2026

1. About EasyHire

EasyHire is the official digital platform of the Public Employment Service Office (PESO) of Iloilo City. It connects jobseekers and employers in Iloilo City and surrounding areas. Use of EasyHire is free for both jobseekers and employers.

2. Account Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to create a jobseeker account. Employer accounts must be created by authorized representatives of a real business or organization registered to operate in the Philippines. PESO reserves the right to verify employer accounts before activating posting privileges.

3. Acceptable Use

By using EasyHire you agree not to:

  • Post false, misleading, or fraudulent information in your profile or job listings
  • Use the platform to harass, intimidate, or discriminate against other users
  • Attempt to access accounts, data, or systems you are not authorized to use
  • Scrape, copy, or redistribute platform content without permission
  • Post jobs that violate Philippine labor laws or PESO recruitment guidelines

4. Content You Submit

You retain ownership of the résumé, profile, and other content you submit. You grant PESO a limited license to display this content on EasyHire for the purpose of matching you with relevant opportunities (jobseekers) or candidates (employers). You can deactivate your account at any time from Settings.

5. Job Matching

EasyHire uses an automated matching engine to surface relevant jobs and candidates based on your profile information. Match scores are guidance, not guarantees. Employment decisions remain the responsibility of the hiring employer; PESO does not employ jobseekers directly through EasyHire.

6. Communication Outside the Platform

When an employer sends you job requirements or an interview schedule, the email is delivered to your contact address. Any communication that takes place outside EasyHire (email replies, phone calls, in-person meetings) is between you and the other party. PESO is not a party to and not responsible for the outcome of those off-platform exchanges.

7. Account Suspension

PESO administrators may suspend or remove accounts that violate these terms, repeatedly receive valid user reports, or are flagged for fraudulent activity. Suspended users will be notified by email at the address on file.

8. Changes to These Terms

PESO may update these terms from time to time as the platform evolves. Continued use of EasyHire after an update constitutes acceptance of the updated terms. Material changes will be announced via the in-app inbox.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to peso.iloilocity@gmail.com or by phone at (033) 337-0800.
