What do the match labels mean?

Every job–jobseeker pairing is scored against four things: education, skills, experience, and certifications. The label is a plain-English summary of how close that pairing lands to a full fit.

{% match_badge 100 %}

The strongest possible fit for your qualifications.

{% match_badge 92 %}

Almost perfect, just a few things missing

{% match_badge 82 %}

More than a few things missing, but the job still fits.

{% match_badge 72 %}

Not the best fit for your qualifications, but still passable.

{% match_badge 30 %}

Not very compatible at all, likely not the right fit for your current qualifications.
